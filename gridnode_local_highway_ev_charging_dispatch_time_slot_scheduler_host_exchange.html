COMPLIANCE DOCUMENT

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026 | Domain: charge.gridnode.in

1. Scope and Data Minimization Principle

GridNode operates a peer-to-peer neighborhood charger registry and public EV charging point dispatch system in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, India). We collect only the minimum operational telemetry required to compute spatial proximity, lock charging time slots, settle electricity tariffs, and issue GST-compliant invoices.

2. Location Telemetry (GPS Coordinates)

When you click "Detect My GPS" or select a highway corridor, your latitude and longitude coordinates are processed locally in your browser to calculate Haversine distance to nearby AC and DC charging points. We do not store continuous background location trails or sell geolocation history to third-party ad networks.

3. Neighborhood Host Address & Schedule Protection

For residential and society peer-to-peer hosts, public map markers display sector-level coordinates, published time-slot windows, and approximate distance. Exact building gate instructions, bay numbers, and host intercom contacts are disclosed only to authenticated EV drivers holding a paid, time-stamped reservation token.

4. Payment & Settlement Data

UPI handles (VPA), card authorization tokens, and settlement bank references are processed via RBI-compliant payment aggregators. GridNode stores only the transaction reference ID, kWh billed, and statutory tax breakdown for seven years as mandated by Indian financial audit regulations.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Drivers and Hosts may export their complete session ledger or request immediate account erasure by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@gridnode.in.

LEGAL AGREEMENT

Terms and Conditions of Use

Last Updated: October 20, 2026 | Governing Law: Mumbai, Maharashtra, India

1. Dual-Role Platform Rules (Drivers & Hosts)

GridNode provides a real-time discovery, time-slot reservation, and settlement interface connecting Electric Vehicle Operators ("Drivers") with Commercial Charging Operators and Residential/Neighborhood Port Owners ("Hosts"). By logging into either role, you agree to abide by the technical safety and scheduling obligations below.

2. Electrical Safety & Connector Compliance

Hosts warrant that any listed charging point (CCS2, Type 2 AC, Bharat DC-001, or 16A 3-Pin Industrial Socket) is wired with dedicated earthing, functional Residual Current Circuit Breakers (RCCB/RCBO), and accurate metering. Drivers must use manufacturer-approved charging cables free of insulation damage or aftermarket adapters not rated for the listed amperage.

3. Scheduled Time Slots & Urgent Charging Hold Window

Hosts may publish specific availability time slots (e.g., 18:00 to 21:00) or mark their port "Available Now". When a Driver books a time slot, that slot is locked exclusively for the booked duration. If the Driver fails to arrive and verify their 6-digit PIN within 20 minutes of the slot start time without notifying the Host, the reservation lock expires and the initial booking hold fee (INR 25) is credited to the Host.

4. Tariff Calculation, Overstay Fees & Refunds

Tariffs are stated in INR per kWh delivered plus applicable GST. If actual energy dispensed is lower than the pre-authorized kWh estimate, the unconsumed balance is automatically refunded to the Driver's original payment method upon session termination. Vehicles occupying a neighborhood driveway port more than 15 minutes past their booked time slot end time incur an idle bay fee of INR 2.00 per minute.

5. Limitation of Liability

Drivers must follow all residential society parking rules and speed limits when entering a Host property. GridNode acts solely as a scheduling and payment intermediary and is not liable for vehicle BMS faults or utility grid outages caused by state distribution companies.
